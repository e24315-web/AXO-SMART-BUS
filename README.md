# AXO-SMART-BUS
SMART CITY
In Sri Lanka's public bus transport domain, passengers can face uncertainty while waiting for a bus: which bus is
approaching, when it will reach their stop, and whether there will be usable space when it arrives. This becomes harder
in rain, fog, darkness, heavy traffic or remote areas where an approaching bus may be difficult to identify. The issue is
also relevant to passengers who cannot depend on visual information. Existing digital services show that live tracking
and journey information are already useful, but a passenger still needs a simple decision-support layer before boarding:
an early identification of the bus, a stop-specific arrival prediction and an indication of expected crowding. Our project
therefore focuses on public bus mobility, passenger information and accessibility, with an initial corridor-level
prototype.
